Our Essence

We are a traditional tavern with years of history serving dishes just like grandma used to make. We don't seek to reinvent gastronomy, but to honor it with generous portions, fresh ingredients, and wood fire.

Our grill is always on, our milanesas are made for sharing, and our service is direct, friendly, and familiar. We want you to feel right at home in your own dining room, but without having to do the dishes.
